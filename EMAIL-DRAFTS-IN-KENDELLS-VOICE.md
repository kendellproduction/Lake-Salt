## Sending checklist (every send, every tier)

- [ ] **Check the "From" field**: must be `contact@lakesalt.us`, NOT your personal Gmail. Click the dropdown if you have multiple accounts.
- [ ] **Personalize the merge fields**: `[NAME]` → first name, `[WEDDING_DATE]` → full date, `[MONTH]` → month name, `[YEAR]` → 4-digit year
- [ ] **Subject line**: short, lowercase-first, no all-caps spam triggers
- [ ] **Spot-check 1-2 emails** per tier before mass-sending
- [ ] **After sending**: update the lead's stage in CRM to `Contacted` (Cowork can automate this)
- [ ] **Schedule Day-10 + Day-28 follow-ups** for each lead (Phase 2: auto-scheduled)

## How to send from contact@lakesalt.us instead of your personal

Gmail web: click your profile picture (top right) → Add another account → sign in as contact@lakesalt.us. Then in any compose window, the "From" dropdown lets you pick. Set contact@lakesalt.us as default by going to **Settings → Accounts → "Send mail as"** and click "make default" next to contact@lakesalt.us.

Mobile Gmail app: tap your profile picture → switch accounts. Each account has its own inbox. Compose from contact@lakesalt.us's inbox to make sure you're sending from there.
