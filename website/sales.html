What You Can Build

Six bars, one craftsman.

Each build starts as a conversation about what you'll serve and where you'll serve it. Then we engineer the bar around it.

Cocktail Bar

Speed-rail, glassware storage, ice well, garnish station — the classic full-service mobile bar.

Coffee Cart

Espresso machine cutout, grinder shelf, water tank, charging — a barista station that travels.

Mocktail / NA Bar

Premium NA mixers, syrups, citrus prep — a full bar experience, zero alcohol.

Dirty Soda Cart

Soda fountains, cream pumps, syrup rail, big-cup storage — a Utah staple, custom built.

Tasting / Wine

Stem storage, marble or stone top, locking cellar, wine bucket cutout. For brands and venues.

Multi-Purpose

Modular insert system — swap a coffee bay for a cocktail bay. One bar, every event you book.

How It Works

From a sketch to a bar that's yours.

Four steps. Roughly 6–10 weeks from approved concept to delivery.

01

Brief

You join the waitlist. Tell us what you'll serve, the look you want, where it'll live, and whether you'll need power.

02

Concept

We sketch a footprint, finishes, internal storage. You approve or iterate. Bring an AI mockup if you have one.

03

Build

Lake Salt fabricates locally. Real wood, real welding, real wiring. Photo updates throughout.

04

Delivery

You pick up at our Saratoga Springs shop, or we deliver locally. We walk you through every drawer.

Built In

Engineered for real events.

Power, On Demand

12V battery + AC outlet capability. Run a blender, a fountain, an espresso machine — no extension cords across the venue.

Built for Movement

Lockable wheels, balanced weight distribution, handle cutouts. One person can roll it, two can lift it.

Yours, Branded

Custom finishes, hidden cable channels, branded face panels, optional marble or stone tops. It looks like your bar — because it is.
