How It Works

Upload your mockup. We build it.

Four steps. Roughly 6–10 weeks from approved mockup to your door.

01

Brief

Join the waitlist. Tell us what you'll serve, the capabilities you need, and where the bar will live.

02

Mockup

You generate an AI image of the bar you want — ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney. We build to that exact image. No back-and-forth.

03

Build

Built in our Las Vegas shop. Real wood, real welding, real wiring. Quick photo confirmation when it's done.

04

Ship

Crated in custom wood crating and shipped to your home or business. Local Vegas? Pickup is on us. Shipping cost is paid at order.

Built In

Engineered for real events.

Power-Ready

Internal wiring routed for an outlet so you can plug in a portable battery (Jackery, EcoFlow, Goal Zero). Run a blender, an espresso machine, LED strips — no extension cords across the venue.

One-Person Liftable

Lockable wheels, balanced weight distribution, handle cutouts. Light enough that one person can lift and load — no crew required.

Yours, Branded

Custom finishes, hidden cable channels, branded face panels, your logo where you want it. It looks like your bar — because it is.
