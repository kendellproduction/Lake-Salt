Our Story

The couple behind
the bar

Lake Salt is Maddie & Kendell Andrews — a husband-and-wife team who turned a love of hosting into Utah's most thoughtful mobile bar experience.

Hospitality is personal to us

We started Lake Salt because we believe the bar is where a celebration comes to life — where your people gather, laugh, and make the toast that everyone remembers. It deserves more than a folding table and a cooler.

So we built a modern mobile bar and a service to match: custom cocktail and mocktail menus designed around your story, planned entirely with you ahead of time, so that on the day itself you don't lift a finger. You buy the alcohol; we handle absolutely everything else — setup, mixers, garnishes, ice, glassware, service, and breakdown.

We're based in Saratoga Springs and we've poured for weddings, corporate events, and backyard parties all across the Wasatch Front. Every event is a little different, and that's exactly how we like it.
