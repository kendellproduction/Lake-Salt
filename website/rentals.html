FAQ

Rental questions.

What's your delivery radius?

We deliver across the Wasatch Front — Salt Lake City, Saratoga Springs, Lehi, Draper, Provo, Park City, and surrounding areas. Local delivery is a flat $10. Pickup from our Saratoga Springs location is always free. Beyond 30 miles, we charge a per-mile add-on on top of the $10 flat — just ask in your inquiry.

Do I need to put down a deposit?

Yes — a refundable damage deposit is required for the mobile bar rental. Tables and chairs typically don't require a deposit unless rented in larger quantities. Final terms are confirmed in your quote.

What happens if something gets damaged?

Light wear from normal event use is expected. Significant damage (cracks, structural damage, deep stains) is deducted from your deposit. We always document the bar's condition before pickup so there's zero ambiguity.

Can I rent the bar with bartenders?

Of course — that's our core service. Head back to the main pricing page for full-service bartending starting around $995.

Will the mobile bar fit in my car?

If you have a larger SUV with the seats folded down, yes. The bar is 70" long × 34" wide × 40" tall. When laid on its side for transport, it's 40" wide — so anything with at least 40" of width and 70" of cargo depth (think Chevy Tahoe, Ford Expedition, Suburban, larger Jeep, full-size pickup) will handle it. Sedan or compact SUV? You'll want our local Wasatch Front delivery instead.

Why are tables limited to 5 and chairs to 30?

That's all the inventory we have for now. We're testing demand before we buy more — if rentals take off, we'll expand. Need more than 5 tables or 30 chairs for your event? Email contact@lakesalt.us and we'll figure something out (we can sometimes source from partners). For context, most local companies charge $9–$10/table and $2.25–$3/chair; our $8 / $2 lands the same 5-table / 30-chair setup at ~$100/day vs ~$114 elsewhere.

How does delivery work?

Pickup at our Saratoga Springs location is always free. Local Wasatch Front delivery is a flat $10 for the bundle (Salt Lake City, Saratoga Springs, Lehi, Draper, Provo, Park City, and surrounding areas). Beyond 30 miles, we charge a per-mile fee on top — just ask in your inquiry and we'll quote it.

How far in advance should I book?

For peak wedding season (June–September), 4–8 weeks is ideal. Off-season, 1–2 weeks is usually fine. Inventory is first-come-first-served, so the sooner the better.
