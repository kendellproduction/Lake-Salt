Wedding questions

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 1–3 months in advance for most events. For peak wedding season (June–September), sooner is better. We always do our best to accommodate shorter timelines — reach out and we'll check availability.

Do you provide the alcohol?

No — Lake Salt is a dry-hire bar service. By Utah law, clients purchase the alcohol. We handle everything else: bartenders, mixers, garnishes, ice, bar tools, setup, and breakdown, and we give you a detailed, guest-count-specific shopping list.

Are your bartenders certified and insured?

Yes — all Lake Salt bartenders are TIPS certified and food-handler certified. We carry full event liability insurance and can provide proof directly to your venue or coordinator on request.

Can you do a mocktail-only or dry wedding?

Absolutely — mocktail and zero-proof events are one of our specialties. We craft elaborate non-alcoholic drinks that are just as impressive as cocktails, ideal for LDS weddings, dry events, and family-friendly celebrations.

What areas do you serve?

The entire Wasatch Front — Salt Lake City, Park City, Lehi, Saratoga Springs, Ogden, Provo, and surrounding areas. Travel within 30 miles of Saratoga Springs is included; beyond that is $1.50/mile round-trip.
