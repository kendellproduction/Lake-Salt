The Honeymoon Spritz

Elderflower, lavender, and prosecco — delicate and romantic

Medium
Difficulty
1
Serves
4 min
Time

Light, floral, and endlessly elegant. This is the cocktail that makes guests say "what IS this?" — and then ask for another immediately.

Ingredients
  • 1 oz St-Germain elderflower liqueur
  • ½ oz Empress 1908 gin (or regular gin)
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ oz lavender simple syrup
  • Top with prosecco
  • Lavender sprig + lemon twist garnish
Method
  1. Add gin, elderflower, lemon, and lavender syrup to a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake lightly for 8 seconds.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe or wine glass.
  4. Top gently with cold prosecco.
  5. Garnish with a fresh lavender sprig and expressed lemon twist.
💡 Pro tip: For a non-alcoholic version, replace gin with seedlip and prosecco with sparkling grape juice.
