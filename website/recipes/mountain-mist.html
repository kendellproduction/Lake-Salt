Mountain Mist

Apple, cinnamon, and ginger — cozy Utah warmth in cold months

Easy
Difficulty
1
Serves
4 min
Time

Perfect for fall and winter events. The spiced apple and ginger create a warming, comforting experience that feels festive without being sugary.

Ingredients
  • 3 oz fresh apple juice (or pressed cider)
  • ½ oz ginger simple syrup
  • ¼ oz cinnamon syrup
  • ½ oz fresh lemon juice
  • Top with sparkling apple juice
  • Cinnamon stick + apple fan garnish
Method
  1. Combine apple juice, ginger syrup, cinnamon syrup, and lemon in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake for 10 seconds.
  3. Pour into a rocks glass over ice.
  4. Top with sparkling apple juice.
  5. Garnish with a cinnamon stick and an apple fan.
💡 Pro tip: Serve warm in a mug for cold outdoor events — omit the ice and top with warm apple cider.
