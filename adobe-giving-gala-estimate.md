## On Brandy in Sangria
Traditional Spanish red sangria typically uses brandy (about 0.5 oz per drink). White wine sangria often skips it. **I removed brandy from this recipe** to simplify your shopping list and reduce cost — the sangria still tastes great with just wine + ginger ale + fresh fruit. If you want a slight upgrade, a small splash of orange liqueur (or peach schnapps) does the same job for less money.
