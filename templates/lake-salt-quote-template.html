Unless this proposal says otherwise, service includes the listed bartending staff, setup and breakdown, standard bar tools and equipment, menu planning, listed cocktail mixers, garnishes, ice, and appropriate standard disposable cups. Lake Salt is a dry-hire bartending service and does not sell or furnish alcohol. Client purchases and owns all alcohol and is responsible for lawful delivery to the venue. Lake Salt provides a shopping guide based on the confirmed menu and attendance. Unopened alcohol remains Client's property.

Client must provide both total guest count and estimated drinking-guest count. Mocktails, sodas, juices, and a self-serve or staffed water station affect supplies, labor, and pricing even when no alcohol is used. Only the beverages and stations listed in the accepted proposal are included.

7. Cancellation and rescheduling

The 10% deposit is non-refundable. For Client cancellation 30 or more days before the event, Lake Salt refunds additional payments except documented nonrecoverable commitments. At 15-29 days, 50% of the service price is due. At 14 days or less, the full service price is due. A reschedule requested 30 or more days out may transfer the deposit once, subject to availability and changed costs; later requests follow the cancellation schedule.

8. Overtime and changes

Changes to attendance, menu, service hours, staffing, venue, stations, rentals, or equipment must be approved in writing. Lake Salt will provide a change order when a change affects price. Event-day overtime requires staff availability and Client or an authorized contact's approval and is billed at {{OVERTIME_RATE}}.

9. Client and venue responsibilities

Client is responsible for accurate event details, venue approval, permits, licenses, power and water access when required, a safe and level service area, and timely access for setup and breakdown. Client must identify an event-day decision maker and disclose venue rules before booking. Delays or restrictions outside Lake Salt's control do not reduce the agreed price.

10. Damage and missing property

Client is responsible only for documented damage or loss caused by Client, guests, vendors, or venue personnel. Client is not responsible for ordinary wear, preexisting damage, or damage caused by Lake Salt's acts or omissions. Lake Salt will provide reasonable documentation of causation and repair or replacement cost before invoicing.

11. Safety and refusal of service

Lake Salt may check identification, refuse alcohol service, and pause or end service when required by law or reasonably necessary for safety. If Client, a guest, vendor, or venue condition causes termination, amounts already due remain due and no refund is owed for unperformed time. If Lake Salt cannot perform for reasons within its control, Client receives a refund of the price allocated to unperformed service, less only completed work accepted by Client.

12. Events beyond either party's control

Neither party is responsible for failure caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control, including severe weather, government orders, utility failure, venue closure, or serious illness. The parties will first try to reschedule or reasonably adjust performance. If that is not possible, Lake Salt refunds payments for unperformed service after subtracting documented work completed and documented nonrecoverable commitments.
