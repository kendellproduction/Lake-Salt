Your questions, answered

How many bartenders?

Two. With ~50 drinking guests, one bartender keeps the cocktail line fast while the second floats — helping pour and keeping the soda, juice, and water station stocked, iced, and clean for all 350 guests throughout the night.

What's included?

Lake Salt supplies the listed cocktail mixers, garnishes, ice, standard disposable cups, bar equipment, setup, breakdown, and menu planning. You purchase the alcohol plus bottled or canned soda, juice, and water at store prices; our shopping guide tells you what to buy and how much. The listed self-serve station labor and supplies cover setup, ice, cups, stocking, and upkeep.

How is pricing calculated?

By drinking guests and scope of service, not total headcount. The full guest count only affects the non-alcoholic station supplies — which is why it's a modest line item, not a multiplier.

Fees & insurance

No travel or service fee is added for the listed scope at Siempre. Approved scope changes, overtime, taxes, or add-ons may change the total. Insurance coverage and any certificate requirements will be verified before booking; this draft does not represent a specific policy or limit.

Alcohol is not included

As a dry-hire service, you purchase the alcohol at store prices — no markup. Once the menu is finalized, we send a simple shopping guide with exactly what to buy and how much.

Booking terms.

These terms are part of this proposal. They keep the responsibilities, timing, and included service clear for the Gilchrist Wedding ("Client") and Lake Salt Bartending ("Lake Salt").

1. Proposal, scope, and estimates

This proposal is based on the event details currently provided, including date, venue, service hours, guest counts, beverage types, and selected package. Any price labeled preliminary is an estimate, not a guaranteed final price. Lake Salt will confirm the final scope and price before booking. Material changes may require a written price adjustment.

2. What Lake Salt includes

Lake Salt supplies the listed staff, setup and breakdown, standard bar tools and equipment, menu planning, listed cocktail mixers, garnishes, ice, and standard disposable cups. Client purchases alcohol and bottled or canned soda, juice, and water. The mobile bar is included if required by Siempre; glassware, rentals, and unlisted stations are not included.

3. Alcohol and dry-hire responsibilities

Lake Salt is a dry-hire bartending service and does not sell or furnish alcohol. Client purchases and owns all alcohol and is responsible for lawful delivery to the venue. Lake Salt provides a shopping guide based on the confirmed menu and attendance. Unopened alcohol remains Client's property.

4. Guest counts and non-alcoholic service

Client must provide both total guest count and estimated drinking-guest count. Mocktails, sodas, juices, and a self-serve or staffed water station affect supplies, labor, and pricing even when no alcohol is used. Only the beverages and stations listed in the accepted proposal are included.

5. Booking and acceptance

The date is not reserved until Lake Salt receives the 10% non-refundable deposit and confirms acceptance. Paying through the proposal's actual payment link means Client accepts the selected package, proposal details, and these booking terms. If the date becomes unavailable before Lake Salt confirms acceptance, Lake Salt will return the payment.

6. Final payment

The remaining balance is due seven calendar days before the event. Lake Salt may pause planning or decline event-day service if payment is overdue. Taxes, approved add-ons, overtime, and change orders are due according to the updated invoice.

7. Cancellation and rescheduling

The 10% deposit is non-refundable. For Client cancellation 30 or more days before the event, additional payments are refunded except documented nonrecoverable commitments. At 15-29 days, 50% of the service price is due. At 14 days or less, the full service price is due. A reschedule requested 30 or more days out may transfer the deposit once, subject to availability and changed costs; later requests follow the cancellation schedule.

8. Overtime and changes

Changes to attendance, menu, hours, staffing, venue, stations, rentals, or equipment require written approval and may change the price. Event-day overtime requires availability and Client or an authorized contact's approval and is billed at $100 per bartender per started hour.

9. Client and venue responsibilities

Client is responsible for accurate event details, venue approval, permits, licenses, power and water access when required, a safe and level service area, and timely access for setup and breakdown. Client must identify an event-day decision maker and disclose venue rules before booking. Delays or restrictions outside Lake Salt's control do not reduce the agreed price.

10. Damage and missing property

Client is responsible only for documented damage or loss caused by Client, guests, vendors, or venue personnel. Client is not responsible for ordinary wear, preexisting damage, or damage caused by Lake Salt. Lake Salt will document causation and reasonable repair or replacement cost before invoicing.

11. Safety and refusal of service

Lake Salt may check identification, refuse alcohol service, and pause or end service when required by law or reasonably necessary for safety. If Client, a guest, vendor, or venue condition causes termination, amounts due remain due. If Lake Salt cannot perform for reasons within its control, Client receives a refund allocated to unperformed service, less completed work accepted by Client.

12. Events beyond either party's control

Neither party is responsible for failure caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control. The parties will first try to reschedule or reasonably adjust performance. If that is not possible, Lake Salt refunds payments for unperformed service after subtracting documented completed work and documented nonrecoverable commitments.
